Nakshatra Slots Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026
Developer: Harsh Alkutkar
Privacy contact: harshalkutkar@gmail.com

Nakshatra Slots is an offline, free-play game. Its credits are fictional points with no cash value. The app does not offer purchases, cash-out, or real-money wagering.

Information stored on your device

The app saves game progress and preferences locally: fictional credit balance, spin and level progress, collected zodiac signs, progressive game counters, music and sound settings, reduced-motion preference, and whether onboarding was completed. This information is used only to restore your game on this device.

Information collection and sharing

The app does not send this saved information or personal information to the developer or any third party. The Android release does not request Internet access and contains no advertising, analytics, or account SDKs. The app does not ask for your name, contacts, precise location, or account credentials. Android requires vibration permission for tactile game feedback.

Retention and deletion

Saved game information remains in the app's private storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Android backup is disabled for this app. You can delete saved information at any time from Android Settings by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app. There is no account or server copy to delete.

Security

Game information stays inside Android's private app storage and is not transmitted by the app. Keep your device's operating system and screen lock up to date.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no personal information is collected or transmitted, there is no child profile or account.

Changes and questions

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before a release that changes those practices. For privacy questions, contact harshalkutkar@gmail.com.
